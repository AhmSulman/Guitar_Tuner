# Tester invitation — send this to each of the 12+

Subject: **Can you install my guitar tuner app? (2 minutes, then forget about it)**

```
Hi —

I've built a guitar tuner for Android and I need 12 people to install it so
Google will let me publish it properly. You do not need to use it or give
feedback. You just need to install it and leave it installed for two weeks.

Two things that matter, or it won't count:

1. Open this link ON YOUR ANDROID PHONE:
   <PASTE THE OPT-IN URL FROM PLAY CONSOLE>

2. You must be signed in with the Google account I invited:
   <THEIR EMAIL ADDRESS>

Tap "Become a tester", then follow the Play Store link and install it.

Please don't uninstall before <DATE + 14 DAYS> — Google resets the counter if
anyone drops out, and I'd have to start the two weeks over.

If you do play guitar, I'd genuinely like to know whether it tunes accurately
for you. But that's a bonus, not the ask.

Thanks
Ahmed
```

## Why the wording is like this

- **"You do not need to use it"** removes the main reason people say no. The requirement
  is that they stay opted in, not that they engage.
- **The two numbered conditions are the whole game.** Most failed opt-ins are someone
  opening the link on a desktop, or being signed into a different Google account than
  the one you listed. Saying it twice is not redundant.
- **Naming the date** makes the two-week commitment concrete and gives you something to
  point at if you need to chase.

## Chasing

Play Console shows how many testers are opted in, but not who. Ask people to reply
"installed" so you can track it yourself. The counter only moves on install — an
accepted invite that was never installed looks identical to nothing from your side.
