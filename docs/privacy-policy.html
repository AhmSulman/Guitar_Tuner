Privacy Policy

Guitar Tuner  ·  com.ahmedsalman.guitartuner  ·  Effective 17 September 2026

Guitar Tuner collects no data. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no network access of any kind. Nothing you play is recorded, stored, or sent anywhere.

Who this covers

This policy applies to the Guitar Tuner application for Android and Windows, published by Ahmed Salman ("we", "us"). It describes how the app handles information — which is, in short, that it does not handle any.

The microphone

Guitar Tuner needs microphone access to do the one thing it does: hear the note you play and tell you whether it is in tune. On Android this is the RECORD_AUDIO permission, which you are asked to grant when you first open the app.

Audio from your microphone is analysed as it arrives and then discarded. Specifically:

  • Audio is held briefly in memory, in fractions of a second, only long enough to estimate the pitch.
  • It is never written to storage. The app contains no code that saves audio to a file.
  • It is never transmitted. See below.
  • No speech recognition, no voice identification, no listening in the background. The microphone is only active while the app is open and running.

You can revoke microphone access at any time in your device settings. The app will stop detecting pitch, which is the whole of its function, but it will not otherwise misbehave.

No network access

This is worth stating precisely, because it is stronger than a promise. The Android build does not declare the INTERNET permission. An Android app without that permission is blocked from opening network connections by the operating system itself — not by our code, and not by our good intentions. The app could not transmit your data even if it tried to.

No module in the app imports any networking library. The app makes no requests to any server, including ours. There is no server.

What is stored on your device

The app keeps one small file, tuner_cache.json, so that it opens the way you left it. It contains exactly four values:

ValueExampleWhy
tuning"Drop C"Reopen on the tuning you last used
selected_string2Remember a locked string
launches7A count of times the app has been opened
last_opened2026-09-17T10:42:45+00:00When it was last opened

This file never leaves your device. It contains no name, no identifier, no location, and nothing that could identify you or your device. It is not readable by us or by anyone else. Deleting or uninstalling the app removes it.

No third parties

Guitar Tuner includes no advertising networks, no analytics or crash-reporting services, and no social or tracking SDKs. Its only runtime components are the Kivy framework and the NumPy numerical library, both of which run entirely on your device and neither of which is given any data by this app.

Because there is no data and no network, there is nothing to sell, share, or disclose — to advertisers, data brokers, or anyone else. We do not and will not do so.

Children

Guitar Tuner is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children. Because it collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children either.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR (Europe and the UK) and the CCPA/CPRA (California) give you rights to access, correct, export, and delete personal data a company holds about you. We hold none. There is no account to close, no profile to export, and no record of you in any system of ours.

The only data associated with this app is the small file on your own device, which is entirely within your control and which uninstalling the app removes.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app introduces anything that collects data — analytics, advertising, or any online feature — this policy will be updated before that version is released, the effective date above will change, and the corresponding Data safety declaration on Google Play will be updated to match. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
